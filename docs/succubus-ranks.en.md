# PSQ Reburn v0.29 · Succubus Ranks and Abilities

Abilities already unlocked remain available as you advance. The table lists abilities and growth **newly gained** at each rank. The titles and ability names below are translations; the names shown in game follow the current Chinese localization.

> The table assumes the default MCM setting that unlocks abilities by succubus rank. Some features can be disabled or customized, and some may require additional mods.

| Rank | Title | New abilities and growth at this rank |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Novice Succubus | **Nudity, Learning, Masturbation**; damage repair is available after installing Apropos 2 and enabling its damage-repair option. |
| **2** | Lesser Succubus | **Phallus, Desire**. |
| **3** | Junior Succubus | **Succubus Drain Health, Succubus Drain Stamina, Succubus Ranged Drain**; unlocks **storage**. |
| **4** | Intermediate Succubus | **Bondage, Fortify Body, Energy Sense**; **racial trait absorption** can be enabled. |
| **5** | Greater Succubus | **Magic Conversion**; **attribute absorption** can be enabled. On a successful absorption, it transfers up to 10 points from the target's highest base attribute maximum. |
| **6** | Succubus Noble | **Attach Phallus, Summon Merchant**; attribute absorption remains limited to up to 10 points from the highest attribute. |
| **7** | Succubus Princess | **Enthrall, Desire Poison**; up to **1 thrall**. Attribute absorption remains limited to up to 10 points from the highest attribute. |
| **8** | Succubus Queen | Up to **2 thralls**; attribute absorption rises to **up to 10 points each** from maximum health, magicka, and stamina. |
| **9** | Succubus Empress | Up to **3 thralls**; attribute absorption rises to **up to 20 points each** from the three maxima. |
| **10** | Succubus Goddess | Up to **4 thralls**; attribute absorption rises to **up to 30 points each** from the three maxima. |

## Bonuses gained by rank

The corresponding MCM settings must be enabled for these values to apply. Custom settings change the actual effects; transformation attribute bonuses apply only while transformed.

| Rank | Normal-form movement speed bonus | Transformed movement speed bonus | Transformed attribute and carry weight bonus | Transformed armor bonus | Spell cost reduction while transformed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0% | 0% | 10% | 50 | 20% |
| 2 | 2% | 4% | 20% | 100 | 40% |
| 3 | 4% | 8% | 30% | 150 | 60% |
| 4 | 6% | 12% | 40% | 200 | 70% |
| 5 | 8% | 16% | 50% | 250 | 80% |
| 6 | 10% | 20% | 60% | 300 | 90% |
| 7 | 12% | 24% | 70% | 350 | 100% |
| 8 | 14% | 28% | 80% | 400 | 100% |
| 9 | 16% | 32% | 90% | 450 | 100% |
| 10 | 18% | 36% | 100% | 500 | 100% |

- **Transformed attributes** means health, magicka, stamina, and carry weight. The percentages use the respective base values. These transformation bonuses are separate from independently configurable permanent abilities.
- **Spell cost reduction** in normal form is half the value shown. It applies to spells in the five schools of magic; it does not necessarily reduce other additional energy costs.
- The actual amount of **attribute absorption** is limited by the target's remaining attributes. Current followers, player teammates, and succubus thralls are protected from permanent absorption of these three attribute maxima.
- **Racial trait absorption** requires its setting to be enabled; **Energy Sense** and **Enthrall** also require their respective abilities to be enabled. Storage depends on the permanent-ability setting, and damage repair requires Apropos 2 and the relevant option.
- Elemental spells available through **Learning** are separate from the automatically unlocked abilities above. Flight, outfits, and other optional features also depend on settings and installed mods.
